Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information SubbieBill collects, why, who it is shared with and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. Who we are

SubbieBill is operated by SubbieBill (ABN 50 115 971 254). For any privacy question or request, email support@subbiebill.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form by our login provider. We cannot see them.
  • Your details for invoices: name, ABN, address, phone, email, and the BSB and account number you want printed on your invoices.
  • Your clients and work sites: client names, client ABNs, client email addresses and site addresses you enter.
  • Your work and invoices: dates, start and finish times, breaks, rates, overtime and public holiday settings, GST settings, expenses, invoice numbers, dates, totals and whether an invoice is paid.
  • Invoice emails: when you use Email to client, the recipient address, the date and time it was sent, and the invoice PDF passed to our email provider to deliver it.
  • Messages you send to our support email.

Information collected automatically

  • The date and time of your last visit, so we can keep the service running and secure.
  • Subscription information from Stripe: your Stripe customer reference, plan (monthly or yearly), status and renewal dates. We never receive your full card number.

3. How we use it

  • to provide SubbieBill: save your hours, calculate totals and create your invoices;
  • to manage your account and keep it secure, including verifying your email address;
  • to apply the Free plan limit, which is one free allowance per ABN;
  • to manage your Premium subscription;
  • to send your invoices to your clients when you ask us to;
  • to answer your questions and send important messages about the service;
  • to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your information. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking tools.

4. Who we share it with

We only share information with service providers that help us run SubbieBill, and only as needed:

  • Supabase: database and login system where your account and invoice data are stored.
  • Stripe: payment processing for Premium.
  • Resend: delivers the invoice emails you send with Email to client (the email, the attached PDF and the addresses).
  • ntfy: sends us a private notification when a new account is created or a Premium subscription starts. It contains the account email address.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of the website.
  • Cloudflare: our domain name and the forwarding of emails sent to our support address.
  • Google: website fonts (Google Fonts) and the mailbox that receives support emails.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States and Japan. We use well-known providers that protect information with security measures such as encryption.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Storage on your device

SubbieBill stores a few small items in your browser: your login session, your language choice and which notices you have closed. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. Security

Connections to SubbieBill are encrypted. Each account can only access its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and invoice data within 30 days, except information we must keep by law. Payment records are kept by Stripe, and email delivery logs by Resend for a limited time, under their own obligations.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see and update your details at any time in Menu → My details, or ask us for a copy of your information.
  • Deletion: email support@subbiebill.com from your account email to ask us to delete your account.
  • Complaints: contact us first and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

9. Information about your clients

When you enter details about your clients, you are responsible for being allowed to use them to invoice those clients. We use them only to create your invoices.

10. Children

SubbieBill is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will tell you by email or in the app.
