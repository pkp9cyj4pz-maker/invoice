How to create an invoice on SubbieBill

Updated 25 October 2026

Every screen, every button. The orange boxes and numbers show exactly what to tap. Setting up takes about 2 minutes the first time; after that an invoice takes about 30 seconds.

Button names are shown exactly as they appear in the app in English. The invoice PDF is always in English.

Set up your account (once)

1Open SubbieBill

Go to subbiebill.com on your phone or computer and tap Create my first invoice.

2Create your account

Tap Create account at the top (or fill in your work first: SubbieBill asks for an account when you finish). Type your email, choose a password, type it again, then tap Create my free account. Already have an account? Tap Log in at the top instead.

3Confirm your email

You get a 6-digit code by email from noreply@subbiebill.com. Type it (1) and tap Confirm my email (2). No email? Check your spam folder.

4Add your name and ABN

In My details, type your First name, Last name and ABN, then tap Confirm my ABN. Check the number and tap Yes, continue. Your ABN is then locked to your account.

5Add your bank details and GST

Type your BSB (1) and Account number (2) so your client knows where to pay you. Under GST, choose Not registered or Registered for GST (3). Then tap Done.

Make an invoice

6Choose your client and period

Under Client, type the business you bill (1). Choose Week, Fortnight or Month (2). Next time, pick the client from the list.

7Choose how you are paid

Under How are you paid?, tap By the hour (1), By the day (2), Fixed weekly (3) or Fixed price (4). The next steps show By the hour. By the day: enter your day rate and tap the days you worked. Fixed amount or price: just type the amount.

8Set your hourly rate

Type your Hourly rate ($/h). It is saved for your next invoices.

9Enter your hours

With Start – finish selected, pick Monday's start (1) and finish (2). Tap Copy Monday to Tue → Fri (3) to fill the week, then change any day that was different.

10Finish the invoice

Check the total at the bottom and tap Finish invoice. Tap Preview if you want to see the PDF first.

11Check and download

Check the invoice summary and tap Finish and download PDF. The invoice gets its number and the PDF downloads.

After the invoice

12Send it to your client

Tap Email to client (1) to email it, or Download PDF (2) to save the PDF and send it yourself.

13Email it (Premium)

Type your client's email (1) and tap Send (2). You get a copy, and your client's reply comes back to you.

14Track your invoices

Tap History at the top of the screen to see every invoice and your totals. When you get paid, tap Mark as paid.

15Invite a friend

Tap the menu (☰) at the top right, then Invite a friend.

16Share your link

Tap Copy and send the link. Your friend gets 1 extra free invoice; you get 1 month of Premium when they subscribe.

Quick answers

How much does it cost?

Your first 3 finished invoices are free. Premium costs A$4.99 a month or A$39 a year and adds unlimited invoices, sending by email and the full history.

Can I change my ABN later?

No. Your ABN is linked to your account for good once you confirm it, so check it carefully.

I didn't get the code by email.

Wait a minute and check your spam or promotions folder. The email comes from noreply@subbiebill.com. You can ask for a new code after 60 seconds.

Can I fix a mistake after finishing?

Yes. Open the invoice and tap Edit. It keeps the same invoice number.
