A spreadsheet template

Good if you invoice rarely and you’re comfortable with formulas.

Watch out for broken formulas, reused invoice numbers, and GST added when you’re not registered. Copying last week’s file and forgetting to change a date is the classic mistake. Our ABN invoice template lists every field you need.

Xero

Good if you run a growing business: employees, bank feeds, BAS lodgement, expenses and an accountant who works in Xero.

Watch out for paying for features you don’t use if all you do is bill your hours to one or two builders. Prices change: check xero.com/au/pricing.

SubbieBill

Good if you’re paid by the hour and want a correct invoice fast: enter start and finish times on your phone, and get a PDF with your ABN, BSB and GST worked out.

Not for bookkeeping, BAS lodgement or payroll. If you need those, use accounting software, or give your SubbieBill invoices to your accountant.
