              Common questions

              What's on the invoice?

              Your name, ABN, contact details, BSB and account number, the business you're billing, the invoice number, dates, a day-by-day breakdown of your hours and the total in AUD.

              Do I need an ABN?

              Yes. As a subcontractor you invoice under your own ABN, and SubbieBill puts it on every invoice. You confirm it once and it stays linked to your account. No ABN yet? Applying is free at abr.gov.au. Without one, the business paying you may have to withhold 47% of the payment.

              How does GST work?

              Tell SubbieBill once, in My details, whether you're registered for GST. Registered: your invoices add GST (10%) on top of your hours, and you can switch it off for an invoice where it doesn't apply. Not registered: no GST is added. Registration is compulsory once your GST turnover reaches $75,000 a year. Check with your accountant if you're unsure.

              Invoice or Tax invoice: what's the difference?

              A “Tax invoice” is for businesses registered for GST: it shows the GST (10%) separately, as the ATO requires. If you're not registered, SubbieBill makes an “Invoice” that says no GST has been charged, because a business that isn't registered must not call its invoice a tax invoice. For tax invoices of $1,000 or more, the client's name or ABN must appear: SubbieBill always prints the client's name.

              How do I download or share my PDF?

              Tap Finish invoice, check the summary, then Finish and download PDF. The PDF goes to your downloads. On most phones, Share PDF sends it straight to Mail, Messages or WhatsApp. Download again works as long as the invoice is open, and from your History with Premium.

              Where are my invoices saved?

              In your SubbieBill account, online, not only on your phone: log in on any device and your details and drafts are there. Drafts save as you type. On Free, your first 3 finished invoices are kept in your History. With Premium, every invoice is kept there.

              How do I change an invoice?

              Until you finish it, change anything: it saves by itself. A finished invoice is locked so your records stay straight. To fix a mistake, open it and tap Edit: correct it and finish it again. It keeps the same number and you get a new PDF to send. On Free, finishing it again uses one of your free invoices.

              What's the difference between Free and Premium?

              Free: 3 finished invoices with every tool included, kept in your History. Premium ($39 a year or $4.99 a month): unlimited invoices, a History of every invoice with yearly totals, emailing invoices to your client, and paid / overdue tracking.

              Who can see my bank details?

              You, and the SubbieBill administrator when helping with your account. Your details are tied to your account, protected by your password and sent over an encrypted connection. Your clients only see them on the invoices you send.

              Can I cancel Premium?

              Yes, anytime. You go back to Free: your History stays in your account and comes back if you return to Premium.
