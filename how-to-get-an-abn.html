How to get an ABN in Australia

Updated 20 October 2026

An Australian Business Number (ABN) is what lets you invoice as a subcontractor. Applying is free, it’s done online, and most people get their number straight away.

Do you need an ABN?

You need an ABN if you work for yourself: you quote or agree a rate, you send invoices, and you look after your own tax and super. If you don’t have one, a business that pays you may have to withhold 47% of the payment and send it to the ATO.

An ABN is for running a business. If you work as an employee, you don’t need one, and having an ABN doesn’t turn an employee into a contractor. Not sure which you are? The ATO has an employee or contractor decision tool.

What you need before you start

  • Your tax file number (TFN).
  • Your personal details and the address you work from.
  • A short description of your work (for example, “carpentry subcontracting”).
  • Your business structure. Most subbies start as a sole trader.

How to apply

  1. Go to the Australian Business Register at abr.gov.au and choose to apply for an ABN.
  2. Answer the questions about you and your work.
  3. Submit. If everything checks out, you’ll usually get your ABN immediately.

If some details need checking by hand, it can take up to 28 days.

It’s free. The Australian Business Register doesn’t charge a fee to apply. Some websites look official but charge you to submit the same form. You don’t need them.

After you get your ABN

  • Check it on ABN Lookup. A new ABN can take a day or two to appear there, so keep your confirmation in the meantime.
  • Decide about GST. You must register once your GST turnover reaches $75,000 a year. Read Do subcontractors charge GST?
  • Put it on every invoice. See the ABN invoice template.
  • Set money aside for tax. No one takes tax out of your pay any more. Many subbies put a fixed share of each payment in a separate account.
  • Think about super. As a sole trader you look after your own super. If you’re paid mainly for your own labour, the business may still have to pay super for you (see the ATO’s guidance on super for contractors).

Quick answers

How much does an ABN cost?

Nothing, if you apply yourself through the Australian Business Register. A tax agent may charge for doing it for you.

Can I have more than one ABN?

As a sole trader you have one ABN, and it covers all your business activities.

Does my ABN expire?

No, but it can be cancelled if you stop running your business. Keep your details up to date on the register.
