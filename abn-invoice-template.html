How to fill it in

  • Your details and ABN at the top. Your ABN is the most important line on the page.
  • Invoice number: unique and in order (1, 2, 3…).
  • Issued and due dates: issue it at the end of the week or period; the due date follows your agreed terms, often 7 days.
  • Bill to: the business that pays you, not the site or the builder’s client.
  • One line per day with the hours after unpaid breaks. Put overtime, weekend or public holiday rates on separate lines.
  • GST: only add it if you are registered. See Do subcontractors charge GST?
  • Bank details: account name, BSB and account number, checked twice.

Template or app?

A Word or Excel template works, but every week you have to update the dates, add up the hours, check the GST and change the invoice number by hand. One mistake can delay your pay. An invoicing app does those steps for you and keeps a copy of each invoice.
