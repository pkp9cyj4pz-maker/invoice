Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

We want you to be happy with SubbieBill. The Free plan lets you create 3 real invoices before you pay anything, so you can check that SubbieBill works for you.

Cancel any time

You can cancel Premium at any time from Menu → Manage my subscription. There is no lock-in contract and no cancellation fee. After you cancel:

  • you keep Premium until the end of the month or year you have already paid for;
  • you will not be charged again;
  • your account then returns to the Free plan and your invoice history stays saved in your account.

When we give refunds

We will refund you in full if:

  • you were charged by mistake, for example charged twice or charged after you had cancelled;
  • Premium did not work because of a problem on our side that we could not fix within a reasonable time.

Apart from these cases and your rights under the Australian Consumer Law below, we do not usually refund a period that has already started, including for a change of mind. We may still choose to help in other situations, so feel free to ask.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email support@subbiebill.com from the email address of your SubbieBill account.
  2. Tell us the date and amount of the payment and why you are asking for a refund.
  3. We reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. For major failures with the service, you are entitled:

  • to cancel your service contract with us; and
  • to a refund for the unused portion, or to compensation for its reduced value.

You are also entitled to be compensated for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. If the failure does not amount to a major failure, you are entitled to have problems with the service rectified in a reasonable time and, if this is not done, to cancel your contract and obtain a refund for the unused portion of the contract.

Nothing in this policy limits these rights.
