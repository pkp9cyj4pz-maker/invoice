Do subcontractors charge GST?

Updated 19 October 2026

Short answer: only if you are registered for GST. Whether you must register depends on how much you earn from your business.

When you must register for GST

You must register for GST when your GST turnover is $75,000 or more a year. That means your business income before expenses, not your profit. It counts if you have already reached $75,000 in the last 12 months, or if you expect to reach it in the next 12 months.

Below $75,000 you can choose to register, but you don’t have to.

If you are not registered

  • Do not add GST to your invoices. You are not allowed to charge it.
  • Call your document an Invoice (not a Tax Invoice).
  • It is good practice to write “No GST has been charged”.
  • Keep an eye on your total income so you know when you get close to $75,000.

If you are registered

  • Add 10% GST to your price and show the GST amount on the invoice.
  • Title it Tax Invoice. For $1,000 or more, include your client’s name or ABN.
  • Report the GST you collected in your Business Activity Statement (BAS) and pay it to the ATO.
  • You can usually claim back the GST included in your business purchases, such as tools and fuel.

Quick answers

Does my client pay me more if I’m registered?

Yes, they pay your price plus 10% GST. A GST-registered client can usually claim that GST back, so it often doesn’t cost them more in the end.

Can I register for GST later?

Yes. You must register within 21 days of reaching the $75,000 threshold. You can register online through the ATO or with a registered tax agent.

What is “GST turnover”?

Your total business income from sales, before taking out expenses, not counting GST itself. It is not your profit.
